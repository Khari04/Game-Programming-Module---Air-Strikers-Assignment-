How to make a sprite sheet.

There is no single "perfect" image size for a sprite, as it depends on the project, but
using a power of 2 for the overall sprite sheet dimensions (e.g., 512x512, 1024x1024) is
highly recommended for performance and compatibility with many game engines. The
individual sprite size within the sheet should be determined by the desired level of detail,
often starting small like 16x16 or 32x32 for pixel art and scaling up for higher
resolutions.
